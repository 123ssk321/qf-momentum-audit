# Research brief

Source idea: #1 in the original ten-idea document. Revised ranking: quick.

## Product and evidence boundary

Research two or three prespecified momentum variants, exposure and cost sensitivity, and all failures. Public factor-series results are educational; an individual-stock alpha claim requires point-in-time universe and delisting data.

## Data path

Public Fama-French series for calculation checks. Public constituent approximations must be labeled survivor-biased. Licensed historical security histories are an optional later gate, not assumed.

## Research evidence

Timestamp contract, fixed hypotheses, lagged features, holdout, turnover, cost sensitivity, factor exposures and complete variant log.

## Product hypothesis and stop rule

A research audit module may have value; a raw momentum signal is unlikely to be a distinct product. Build as a research case using Trading Lab facilities, not a new backtester. If only current constituents or unverifiable corporate actions are available, publish method validation and data-limit analysis instead of stock-level alpha claims.
